Ten signals worth paying attention to each month. Curated from books, white papers, research, and the knowledge graph behind the published work. Each issue includes a short editorial note on what the signals collectively point to, followed by the ten items.

Not trends for the sake of trends. Not recaps of what everyone is already talking about. Signals that matter for architects, engineering leaders, and teams building serious software systems.

Published monthly.
